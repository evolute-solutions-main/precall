04 / THE PROBLEM

Scaling A Contracting Co Is Simple

It comes down to two things: generating leads and turning them into appointments. That's the main driver of your business's growth — and we handle 100% of it, so all you have to do is show up to the appointments we schedule.

PROBLEM: Relying on word-of-mouth

Even if you have a huge month, next month could look completely different. Growth plateaus, and you're stuck working smaller projects just to fill gaps.

SOLUTION: Consistent lead generation

Our system delivers a steady flow of your ideal projects every month, so you can hire confidently and focus on the most profitable work.

PROBLEM: Buying shared leads

Leads shop around for the lowest price, your info is shared with competitors, and many leads are suspicious or low quality.

SOLUTION: Your own exclusive leads

We run ads under YOUR brand. When someone responds, they're asking for YOU specifically — not shopping 5 contractors at once.

PROBLEM: Wasting time chasing leads

You're a business owner, not a cold caller. Most contractors have no idea how many touchpoints it takes to actually convert a lead.

SOLUTION: AI + human follow-up

Our AI and human team handle every call, text, and email — sometimes following up for 3+ weeks until a lead is ready to schedule.

PROBLEM: Meeting cold prospects

Without prep, leads forget appointments, you show up as a stranger, and you're doing 100% of the selling on the spot.

SOLUTION: Pre-appointment indoctrination

We send your best work, reviews, and differentiators BEFORE the meeting. When you arrive, they already trust you and see your value.

09 / FAQ

Questions You're Probably Asking

How hard is it to get my first close?
Close rates depend on how good you are at closing in-home. Most of our partners run 25-45% when the system is dialed in. Your job is to show up to qualified, pre-scheduled homeowners and do what you already do best — close deals. Our job is to get you in front of them.
How much is the program?
Pricing depends on the volume of appointments you need and your area (appointments in Beverly Hills cost more to schedule than in rural Arkansas). I'll walk you through exact pricing for your specific situation on our call once I understand your goals and market.
I don't know AI or marketing — is this for me?
Yeah, most of our partners don't either — and that's fine. That's literally what you're paying us for. We handle all the complex tech, ads, follow-up, qualification, and scheduling. All we need you to do is be on top of the appointments we book and close deals. Trust the process — my partners do well because this stuff works.
What does ad tracking look like?
Our ad tracking is direct, which is more accurate than any platform can deliver on its own. We track conversions end-to-end so our ads optimize around the leads that actually make you money — not just the cheapest clicks. You'll never have to look at any of this yourself. I'm just flexing the fact that we know our shit.
How long until I start seeing results?
Onboarding is fast, and most of our partners start seeing leads within the first week of going live. Appointments follow shortly after. Full results compound over the first 30-60 days as the AI learns your business and the ad data sharpens.
What if this doesn't work in my market?
Every market has homeowners who need your service — that's why your business exists in that market in the first place. We've gotten contractors results everywhere from major metros to rural areas. The system works because we're not relying on someone randomly stumbling across your ad. We're actively reaching out, qualifying, and booking on your behalf.
Do I have to spend extra on ads?
Ad spend is included as part of your program — we handle all of it. You're not paying separately for ads on top of the program fee. Full details on the call.
